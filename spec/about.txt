spec/ — sentences and invariants

Files
  spec.yaml        one EARS sentence per rule
  const.yaml       every invariant bound to one check
  decisionTable/   the exhaustive dispatch table
  about.txt        this page

Rules
  spec reads only intent. const reads intent, spec and the decision table.
  One shall is one assertion. No should, may, 可以, 快速, 支持, 处理, 等, 尽量.
  An unknown condition goes back to intent as a question, not into spec.
  Every constraint names one check; no check means delete the constraint.
  A counterexample writes never, not shall.

Exit
  Every rule has at least one shall.
  Every constraint has a check and a trace.
  Every undecided requirement names its blocking question.
