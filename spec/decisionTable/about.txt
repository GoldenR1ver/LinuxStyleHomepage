decisionTable/ — one table per scenario

File
  decisions.csv    YAML inside a .csv name; one or more tables separated by ---

Rules
  Every combination of the conditions has a row. No otherwise.
  A dash means the condition does not change the result.
  unique means rows do not overlap; first means row order is priority.
  A rejection action carries a code.
  A condition that does not change the result is removed, not kept.

Audit three questions
  Missing: does every combination have a row?
  Overlap: on a unique table, can two rows hit the same combination?
  Dead: is a row unreachable?

Exit
  G-DT-01 (next round) reports zero missing, overlapping, dead, or unknown rows.
